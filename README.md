## 🕹️ About

Keep the Witch Out of This Inn is an adult fantasy adventure and deduction simulation developed by StudioVR and published by HENTAI Plan and OTAKU Plan. Released for Windows on September 19, 2026, it places you in charge of an inn threatened by witches disguised as human guests. Each day, you question newcomers, perform inspections, manage limited action points, and compare their reactions to identify who is dangerous before nightfall. Randomized identities, multiple difficulty levels, 21 guest characters, branching outcomes, and extensive animated story content give each playthrough a distinct investigative challenge.

Ideal for players who enjoy mature fantasy adventures, deduction games, inn management, and replayable character-driven simulations.

## ✨ Features

- 🎞️ **Animated Story Scenes** — Experience fully voiced mature scenes with animated presentation and extensive CG content.
- 🏚️ **Innkeeper Simulation** — Protect your inn by making careful decisions throughout each day and night cycle.
- 🕵️ **Witch Deduction** — Use conversations and inspections to distinguish disguised witches from human guests.
- 👥 **21 Guest Characters** — Meet a large cast of distinct female adventurers with different reactions and story content.
- 🎲 **Randomized Identities** — Changing witch identities and events make repeated playthroughs less predictable.
- ⚔️ **Multiple Difficulties** — Choose a challenge level that changes how much evidence is available during investigations.
- ⏳ **Limited Action Points** — Spend your time and resources wisely when investigating multiple suspicious guests.
